How we handle your information

Short version: Kalivae only collects what it needs, keeps your health answers on your own device, and never sells your email. The rest of this page explains exactly what that means.

What we collect

When you finish the Longevity Protocol quiz and submit it, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • The date and time you submitted the quiz
  • Your goals (whichever of Strength & muscle, Better sleep, Healthy skin aging, Longevity basics, and Fill nutrition gaps you picked)
  • Your diet type (omnivore, vegetarian or vegan)
  • Whether you ticked the newsletter opt-in box and, if you did, the exact date and time you ticked it

That's everything we transmit off your device when you submit the quiz. Nothing else.

What stays on your device

The quiz asks you a handful of other questions to tailor the plan we show you on the results screen — your sleep quality, any skin concerns, how much daylight you get, how you'd describe your protein intake, and a short list of safety checks (pregnancy, blood thinners, kidney disease). We use those answers only in your browser, to compute your personalized plan.

Those answers never leave your device. They are not sent to Kalivae, to Formspree, or to any other service. If you refresh the page, they are gone.

Why we collect it

The email and the two or three short pieces of lifestyle context above exist so Kalivae can:

  • Send you the Kalivae newsletter if you asked for it. We never send you marketing email unless you ticked the newsletter box and the timestamp recorded that consent.
  • Group future newsletters by goal, so someone who chose "Better sleep" doesn't get a message that's all about creatine.
  • Reach out to you if we need to correct something we published that you relied on.

That's it. We do not use your information to train advertising profiles, and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

Your quiz submission is received by Formspree, a form-handling service based in the United States. Formspree stores the submission and emails it to Kalivae. Their privacy policy governs their side of that processing; our relationship with them is a standard data-processing one.

When Kalivae moves its newsletter to a dedicated email service (we expect to use an email platform such as Kit or Beehiiv), we will add that service here and give you the chance to opt out before the move.

Beyond those processors, we don't share your email or your quiz answers with anyone. We do not sell data. We do not pass it to advertisers. We do not pass it to brands whose products appear on the site, even when we later earn commission on them.

Kids

Kalivae is not aimed at anyone under 18 and we don't knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a child has submitted the quiz, email us and we'll delete the record.

Your choices and your rights

  • Unsubscribe: every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Unsubscribing stops marketing email immediately.
  • Delete your data: email us and ask us to delete your submission. We'll do it within 30 days and confirm when it's done.
  • See what we have: email us and ask for a copy of what we hold about you. Same 30-day window.
  • EU/UK and California residents have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA — right of access, correction, deletion, portability, and the right to opt out of any sale of personal information (we don't sell any). Use the same contact address and we'll handle it.

Cookies and tracking

Kalivae doesn't set any tracking cookies or use third-party analytics such as Google Analytics. If that changes we'll update this section and ask for cookie consent in a banner before setting anything.

The quiz uses your browser's local storage only to remember your answers in-session, so you don't lose them if the submission fails and you need to try again. Nothing from local storage is sent anywhere.

Contact us

For anything to do with privacy, write to hello@kalivae.com (email to be configured — until then, please use the contact link in the newsletter or the GitHub repository's issue tracker). We aim to reply within three business days.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page, change the "last updated" date at the top, and — if the change is significant — email anyone who's subscribed to the newsletter and give them a chance to opt out before the change takes effect.

This policy is a plain-English statement of Kalivae's actual practices as of the date above. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a lawyer in the jurisdictions where Kalivae collects personal information before it is relied on as a legal document.
