Affiliate disclosure

How Kalivae makes money

We make this page easy to find and update it whenever our commercial relationships change, because a site that reviews products has a duty to say plainly how the money works.

Last updated 5 November 2026

Where we are today

Kalivae is not currently enrolled in any affiliate program and does not earn a commission on any purchase you make through the site. The product links in our picks row go to each merchant's own product page with no tracking tag attached. We have applied to Awin and CJ Affiliate and are waiting on approvals. Nothing on the site is monetised yet.

If you came here from a product card: clicking the "View at Life Extension" or "View at AG1" button right now gives Kalivae nothing. It is simply the merchant's own page. We say this plainly on the picks row and in the quiz results.

What will change when we're approved

Once a program accepts Kalivae, any link we have that goes to one of that brand's products will become an affiliate-tracked link. When that happens, we'll do three things:

  • Mark the button on that card with a short "affiliate link" note, so you can see at a glance which links pay Kalivae if you buy.
  • Update this page with the list of active programs and the brands they cover.
  • Show a short disclosure line close to the first affiliate link on every page that has one, so you don't have to come back here to see it.

A link to this page will stay in the footer of every page, including the homepage and the quiz results, so it is always one tap away.

What commission does and doesn't change

Commissions are paid by the merchant, not by you. The price you pay is the same whether you click through from Kalivae, type the address in directly, or use another website entirely.

What commission does not change on Kalivae:

  • Our evidence ratings. Gold, silver and bronze are set by the recommendation engine based on the quality, size and consistency of the human evidence. A brand cannot pay to move from bronze to silver.
  • Our product selection. We choose picks based on evidence quality for the ingredient, independent third-party testing, and a fair price per dose. A brand cannot pay to appear.
  • What we will tell you to skip. When the honest answer is "you don't need this", we say so, even for brands we earn commission from.

If we ever accept a payment from a brand for placement (we do not plan to), the content involved will be labelled "Sponsored" clearly, and it will not be rated using the gold / silver / bronze system.

The Kalivae Recommended badge

The faceted-gem "Kalivae Recommended" badge is independent of any commercial relationship with the brand behind the product. To earn it, a product needs gold-rated evidence for what it claims, independent third-party testing, and a fair price for the dose that was studied. No brand can buy the badge, and we will remove it if any of the three criteria stop being true.

Reviews written before affiliate programs go live

The first wave of reviews on Kalivae is being written before we have active affiliate relationships. That means the ratings and picks here today were set purely on the evidence. When affiliate links are added later, the rating on those products will stay exactly as it was set. If the evidence changes, we re-rate, show the new grade, and say in the article what changed and when.

FTC compliance note

The United States Federal Trade Commission requires websites to disclose a "material connection" with any brand when that connection isn't obvious. Once Kalivae earns commission on any link, that is a material connection. We disclose it on the page where the link appears, not only here, so that every visitor sees the disclosure near the recommendation itself. This page is the longer version of that disclosure.

Questions, corrections, or concerns

If you think a page on Kalivae has an affiliate link that isn't labelled, or a product that was placed because of a commercial relationship rather than its evidence, write to hello@kalivae.com (email to be configured). We take that seriously and will act on it.

This disclosure reflects Kalivae's actual commercial status as of the date above. We'll update this page whenever that status changes.
