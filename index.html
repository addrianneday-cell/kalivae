How our ratings work

Research in. A verdict out.

One

We go to the source

Human trials and reviews of many studies come first. Animal and lab studies are always labelled, so you know what's actually been shown in people.

Two

We weigh the evidence

How many studies, how big, how long, and whether they agree. That decides the rating.

Three

We give you a verdict

Who it's for, who should skip it, and the dose that was actually studied. Sometimes the answer is that you don't need it.

Strong

Gold · strong evidence

Several well-designed human trials, with results that agree.

Moderate

Silver · moderate evidence

Some human data, but small, short or mixed. Promising, not settled.

Limited

Bronze · limited evidence

Mostly animal, lab or anecdotal. Interesting, but not yet shown in people.

Questions

Good questions deserve sources

Can't find your answer? Write to us.

What is Kalivae?

An independent site about longevity, skin health and supplements. We read the research, rate the evidence, and tell you plainly what's worth your time and money.

What do gold, silver and bronze mean?

Gold means strong evidence: several well-designed human trials that agree. Silver means moderate evidence: some human data that's small, short or mixed. Bronze means limited evidence: mostly animal, lab or anecdotal.

What does "Kalivae Recommended" mean?

It's our single top pick in a category. To earn the diamond, a product needs gold-rated evidence for what it claims, independent third-party testing, and a fair price for the dose you actually need.

How do you make money?

Through affiliate commissions when readers buy products we recommend. Brands can't pay for a rating, and we'll tell you to skip a product when the evidence says so.

Is this medical advice?

No. Kalivae is educational. Talk to your clinician before starting a new supplement or routine, especially if you're pregnant, nursing or take medication.
