A small 2015 randomized clinical trial found that a structured mindfulness program improved sleep quality and daytime problems more than sleep-hygiene education in older adults with sleep disturbances. That study evaluated a mindfulness program, not a specific branded headset or flashing-light system.

**Our take:** A good relaxation habit can be useful without extraordinary device promises.
