**Subject:** Infrared Sauna Blankets: Relaxing Ritual or Longevity Breakthrough?

**Preview:** An infrared sauna blanket can turn an evening at home into a warm little ritual. The lifespan claims deserve a colder look.

An infrared sauna blanket can turn an evening at home into a warm little ritual. The lifespan claims deserve a colder look.

Some observational research links regular sauna use with favorable cardiovascular outcomes. Those studies cannot prove that sauna bathing caused the benefit, and they generally investigate traditional sauna practices, not home infrared blankets. A 2025 systematic review of randomized passive-heating trials found no significant overall effect on most measured cardiometabolic markers.

**Our take:** A sauna blanket may be a lovely ritual; it is not a proven longevity treatment.
