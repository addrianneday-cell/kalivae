# Newsletter — Magnesium for Sleep: Helpful Mineral or Overhyped Bedtime Ritual?

**Subject:** Magnesium for Sleep: Helpful Mineral or Overhyped Bedtime Ritual?

**Preview:** Magnesium has become a bedtime staple. The science is more interesting, and less certain, than the marketing suggests.

Magnesium has become a bedtime staple. The science is more interesting, and less certain, than the marketing suggests.

A systematic review of three randomized trials in 151 older adults found that supplemental magnesium shortened the time it took to fall asleep by about 17 minutes on average. That sounds encouraging until you read the next sentence: the studies had substantial limitations, and the overall certainty of evidence was low to very low. The results also do not establish that magnesium glycinate specifically has a clinically meaningful benefit.

**Our take:** Magnesium is useful. Calling it an insomnia cure goes beyond the evidence.
