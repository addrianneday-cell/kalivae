Red light masks are everywhere. The better question isn’t whether they glow, but whether a particular device has evidence behind it.

A 2025 randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled study of a home-use mask using 630-nanometer red and 850-nanometer near-infrared light reported improvements in crow’s-feet assessments over 16 weeks. A 2026 systematic review of eight at-home studies including 365 participants also found encouraging cosmetic signals, while emphasizing inconsistency across treatment methods and the need for larger trials.

**Our take:** Red light is intriguing, but the exact device and claim matter.
