# Newsletter — Massage Guns: Do They Actually Help Recovery?

**Subject:** Massage Guns: Do They Actually Help Recovery?

**Preview:** A massage gun can feel great after a hard day. That doesn’t necessarily mean it is rebuilding your muscles faster.

A massage gun can feel great after a hard day. That doesn’t necessarily mean it is rebuilding your muscles faster.

A 2023 systematic review of 11 studies found some evidence for short-term flexibility and range-of-motion benefits. It did not consistently support strength, balance, speed or explosive-performance improvements. A later review of randomized trials found uncertain results for soreness and maximal strength recovery, even when some performance markers improved.

**Our take:** Think of massage guns as optional comfort tools, not guaranteed recovery shortcuts.
