# Newsletter — Retinol, Retinal and Tretinoin: What Really Works for Photoaging?

**Subject:** Retinol, Retinal and Tretinoin: What Really Works for Photoaging?

**Preview:** The skincare aisle can make every vitamin A product sound identical. They aren’t.

The skincare aisle can make every vitamin A product sound identical. They aren’t.

A 2025 systematic review and meta-analysis pooled eight randomized trials involving 1,361 participants and found that topical tretinoin improved both fine and coarse wrinkles associated with photodamage compared with inactive vehicle products. Other reviews also support improvements in certain uneven pigmentation and texture outcomes.

**Our take:** The most proven skincare ingredient is not always the flashiest one.
